# The future of ClearCut

Since we launched the first version, our mission has always been clear: to provide powerful, free, and **completely private** image editing tools.

Today, we are excited to share a sneak peek of what version 2.0 will bring.

## Integrated Magic Eraser

One of the most requested features has been the ability to remove unwanted objects from photographs. Thanks to recent advances in artificial intelligence models that run directly in the browser (using WebAssembly and WebGL), this will finally be possible without sacrificing your privacy.

### New features

- **Object Removal:** Select anything in your photo and it will disappear as if by magic.
- **SVG Optimizer:** Clean up junk code generated by vector tools.
- **Metadata Cleaning (EXIF):** Remove sensitive information (such as GPS location) from your photos before sharing them.

We are working hard to make these features available as soon as possible. Stay tuned to our social networks for more updates!
